What it does

Furlo is a reusable pet hair roller. Its angled bristles comb fur out of fabric and sweep it into a sealed chamber. Open the lid, tip it out, and go again. No sticky sheets, no batteries.

Size: 30 × 11 cm. Weight: 210 g.

How to use

1. Roll back and forth in short strokes. 2. Open the lid. 3. Tip the fur into the bin. Rinse the head under the tap whenever it needs a refresh.

Questions? Answered.

Does it work on cat hair and short dog hair?

Yes. The angled bristles grab long, short and fine fur. Short, stiff hair takes a few more passes in both directions.

Will it damage my couch?

No. There is no suction, heat or blade. It's safe on fabric sofas, beds, rugs and car seats. Skip leather and silk.

How do I clean it?

Open the lid and tip the fur into the bin. Rinse the bristle head under the tap when it needs a refresh and let it dry.

How long does shipping take?

Orders leave our US warehouse in 1–2 business days and usually arrive in 5–9 business days. You get tracking by email.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Email us within 60 days of delivery and we refund you in full. No forms, no hassle.

Does it work on clothes?

It works on most fabrics, but it's built for furniture-sized areas. For clothes, lay them flat and use short strokes.
